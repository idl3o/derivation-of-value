## 0. The position in one paragraph

One rule — derive the staked quantity rather than borrow it — applied once per document across thirty-four documents, this companion aside, and measured wherever a measurement could be built. The rule survives. Almost nothing it was first applied to does. Every substrate the anthology named met the same wall, and the wall was attestation; the attestation problem reduced to residue; residue, measured, is bought entirely by anchors; the anchors are keys; copies are paid as one witness until, at half the identities, the copies are the world; and the one reading that evidences anything beyond its own anchor measures contact with a world the mechanism does not control, priced on a boundary the adversary can shrink to nothing by choosing the level at which to lie. What remains when the keys are removed is an obstruction class of dimension one. The corpus names it value. Nothing is built. The security proof is further off than at any previous journal. These are the facts, and the program continues from them.

## 1. The rule

Every consensus protocol stakes something. Bitcoin stakes energy; proof of stake stakes capital; proof of useful work stakes a product and fails because the product needs an oracle. In each case the staked quantity is borrowed from a market, a regime, or an institution with a half-life. The program's single operator is: stake what those quantities are derived *from* — structural agreement, negentropy, dimensional integrity, capacity — and verify it by many witnesses checking each other rather than by anyone's testimony.

Volume I named the operator. Everything after it is the operator applied once and the result recorded, including the results that went the wrong way. The register is correction-in-place: struck text stays visible, and a refuted claim is not deleted but marked with the paper that refuted it.

## 2. The corpus, document by document

Thirty-two documents in `_papers/` as of 7 November 2026 — the twenty-eight of commit `8f1de6c` (20 September 2026), the three papers of 10 August 2026 admitted from an unmerged branch as orders 29–31, and *The Census of Errors* (order 32, drafted 3 October 2026 and recovered) — plus the two papers of 6 November 2026 that enter the repository with this companion as orders 33 and 34. ### 2.1 The anthology (five volumes)

**Volume I — Derivation of Value** (order 1, working draft). Names the operator. Reads proof of stake as "a bonfire in slow motion"; reads useful work's verification problem as the consensus problem in costume; sketches coherence as four conditions (Tarski, consistency, Solomonoff, Madhyamaka) recovered as vanishing cohomology. Promises four substrates. Carries one sentence — that negentropy "escapes the first two derivations" — which *The Barrowverse* now records as unsupported. Material for a Volume VI.

**Volume II — Proof of Preservation** (order 5, v0.1). The negentropy derivation. The second law guarantees order is scarce and refuses to attest it: Landauer certifies that energy was spent, never that order was made here. Four forger freedoms (backdating, grinding, duplication, free identity minting). Preservation cannot be proved, only certified by fixing those freedoms. Hands the construction to *Gauge-Fixing*.

**Volume II — Omnium** (order 7, v0.1). Value as a vector; the scalar price is a projection "performed by the violence of accounting." Five algebraic kinds of coordinate; asymmetric conversion fees as thermodynamics inside the instrument. A kernel of a few thousand lines running two economies. First claim of a derivation surviving contact with a machine. Admits the independence of its own dimensions is unmeasured.

**Volume II — Kar-Coin** (order 8, v0.4). Capacity as substrate, denominated on Kardashev's outward scale and Barrow's inward one. Outward capacity is oracle-attested and collapses; the inward lattice "is its own witness." Hybrid post-quantum signatures required from page one. The intrinsic verifier for capacity "is not yet written." v0.4 notes that one outward rung attests itself: the hash residue of proof of work.

**Volume III — Admitted or Refused** (order 9, v0.2). The turn from derivation to checking. The vectorised-money kernel proves thermodynamics (conservation, entropy direction, substrate independence) under hundreds of tests; it does not prove Goodhart-asymptotic security, which "needs an adversary in the loop." The first conjecture handed to a machine: richness is a fractional spectral dimension (*admitted*, d_s ≈ 1.61); nested complexes carry a gap hierarchy (*refused* — a degeneracy artefact). Designed to stay open.

**Volume IV — Borrowed Hardness** (order 10, v0.3). Durability. Shor dissolves structured (borrowed) hardness; Grover only taxes unstructured (derived) hardness. Substrate-by-substrate audit: the VDF delay chain in RSA groups is "the single most quantum-fragile load-bearing piece." Declares the visible spine complete (name → apply → construct → endure) and the anthology not closed.

**Volume V — What Cannot Be Helped** (order 15, v0.2). The deflation. Every substrate met the same wall and the wall was attestation. The line between what an action *asserts* and what it cannot help but *leave* — residue — was drawn four times without being noticed. Residue is negentropy with its provenance fixed; the program named fewer substrates than it thought. Residue answers forgery and says nothing about denial. Soundness ("no trace without the work") is left as a design obligation that *Sign and Work* then shows cannot be discharged on coherence.

### 2.2 The framework and the whitepaper

**Combination Proofs** (order 2, v0.7). Goodhart as a property of the substrate, not the proxy. Reward gated on the conjunction of K intrinsically verifiable, approximately independent projections; fake-cost multiplies (Prop 3.1, not a theorem); publicity-positive security. Introduces residue, harvestable projections, richness ρ, Conjecture 7.4 (universality, "almost certainly overreaching"). Revised in place six times. Its one worked instance has collapsed toward K = 1: the spectral projection is not independent of the kernel (ι ≈ 0.08), the temporal projection is independent (ι = 1.000) but free to forge. The sharpest open problem is whether any projection can be both independent and expensive.

**Proof of Coherence** (order 3, v0.5). The sheaf mechanism over the miner–validator–task complex; coherence as vanishing H¹; reward by marginal removal; Proof by Resonance as the spectral extension. v0.5 abstract: the spectral extension is retained, its claimed security benefits are not. A frozen cluster outscores the honest network fivefold. Under output anchors the cheapest forgery is free. "The coherence layer contributes nothing to forgery resistance." The construction stands as a gluing formalism and a diagnostic; its security is attributed to its anchors.

**Onboarding** (order 4, v0.1). Non-technical front door. Written against an earlier whitepaper; does not reflect v0.3–v0.5.

### 2.3 The papers — the anchor and reading thread

**Gauge-Fixing the Section Space** (order 6, v0.4). Four anchors, each fixing one gauge freedom: beacon (grinding), delay chain (temporal), unique encoding (identity), dissipation floor (multiplicity). The security reduction becomes "no coalition can lie consistently across all overlaps." Measured: with unique encoding removed the cheapest non-truthful section is free; pinning every vertex gives τ ≤ 1 − b₁/\|E\|; "the trace gap is bought entirely by the anchors." Spectral gap on the program's hierarchical cover λ₂ = 0.0038; the coalition's price "is the cut, not the gap." The full conjunction has never been assembled.

**Sign and Work** (order 16, v0.4). Stigmergy; sematectonic versus marker-based traces. Defines the trace gap τ = f/w (forging cost over earning cost); soundness ⇔ τ ≥ 1. Calibrated on proof of work at 1.054. Proves the output-anchor ceiling τ ≤ 1 − b₁/\|E\| and the generative-anchor limit τ < 1. "Coherence never contributes to the trace gap." Richness is paid for in trace gap. Cited by more of the corpus than any other document.

**Independent and Expensive** (order 20, v0.3). The exclusion principle: independence and trace gap are claims on one finite budget of paid work, so no two projections of one substrate are both fully sound and fully independent (τ₁ + ι(2\|1)·τ₂ ≤ 1). The only escape is an anchor per projection. "A Combination Proof of order K is K anchors wearing readings." Full stasis scores 9.135× honest persistence: the anchor eats the signal.

**The Second Pool** (order 21, v0.3). Builds the second anchor. Under induced restriction maps every eigenspace-overlap reading measures only uniform frame motion, which is a time-dependent gauge, so coherent change and stasis score identically — survival of the reading indistinguishable from zero. The transition anchor buys ι = 1 both ways, exactly as the arithmetic predicted, and the purchased projection evidences nothing beyond its own anchor. Names the *idle* projection as a third way richness buys nothing.

**One's Own Anchor** (order 22, v0.4). Native share τ_N = ι(π\|π_A)·τ. Theorem 3.4: every declared-frame reading evidences nothing. The one reading outside the class — Procrustes-fitted restriction maps gated on residuals — evidences the world's innovation since the last public record, and only on a coalition's boundary. With public overlaps the boundary is a free ride; with commit-before-compare it is the price. Block coalitions of 16/32/64/128 on the specimen have 3/2/3/2 boundary edges. The corpus's one surviving reading, and what it measures is contact, not work.

**The Budget Across Levels** (order 23, v0.2). Receipts sum, interfaces nest. The evidencing budget of a holarchy is its finest level's. A block coalition's boundary halves per level exactly; an adversary free to pick a level pays zero native work. *Gluing the Gates* Prop 6.1 receives its measured instance: the minimum over levels is zero. The Sybil criterion is correct and not readable from the residuals.

**Every Basis at Once** (order 26, v0.1). The quantum reading divided in two. Commitment makes a participant non-contextual by construction (Fine's theorem with commitment as hidden variable), so contextuality marks only the uncommitted adaptive copier, refused by a free self-consistency gate. What the beacon really buys is the *cover*: redrawing who-compares-with-whom makes a coalition's boundary a random cut, restoring the Sybil floor to half of honest, at the cost of the substrate's spectral dimension. "The cover is the lever." The design curve is a table, not a solution.

**A World with Memory** (order 27, v0.1). Against the optimal record-only attacker (the Kalman filter on the public record), the residual reading's price is the world's innovation given the record; a world with momentum shrinks it until the coalition passes at zero derivations and then outscores honest. The pool is conditional entropy; ι and τ are ratios of conditional information and meet all four of the corpus's constraints in one line each. The gate's resolution is the honest derivation variance. The surviving positive claim is "bounded by a property of the world the mechanism does not control."

**The Wound Clock** (order 29, v0.1, written 10 August 2026, admitted 7 November). An amortisation audit of the four anchors. Perpetuity redefined as cost with no half-life; the beacon is sky-funded, the floor genesis-paid, memory a constant-floor at the Landauer wage, and the delay chain and unique encoding *wound* — sequential time spent freshly every epoch. The class that must be paid forever is the anchor *Borrowed Hardness* marked most quantum-fragile and the one *Sign and Work* found the trace gap bought at: the winding point is the wound. Predates the exclusion line and was not revised against it.

### 2.4 The papers — the composition and contextuality thread

**Gluing the Gates** (order 11, v0.3). Koestler's holon as a cellular sheaf. Conjunction-gating does not survive nesting; the obstruction is cohomological (for affine gates, a Čech class); absent gluing, security is the minimum over levels rather than the product. Non-compositional holarchy identified with Abramsky–Brandenburger contextuality.

**No Global Section** (order 13, v0.4). Composition failure *is* contextuality. Three strengths (probabilistic, logical, strong) grade it. The cohomological obstruction is sufficient, not necessary: "a detector of composition failure, not a certificate of composition safety."

**The Obstruction, Computed** (order 24, v0.4). Conjecture 3.1 holds: the Čech obstruction applies to gate scenarios with no affine hypothesis and no distribution chosen. Calibrated on the literature's answer key with zero false positives in 31,000 sections. On cyclic holarchies with spectral gates every strongly contextual model is seen and 97 of 233 logically contextual ones are missed. On a cyclic cover the obstruction is undirected reachability in the bundle diagram, ring-independent. Design rule: no tolerance gate in any chain. v0.4 (29 October 2026) has Theorem 6.3 and Corollary 6.4 machine-checked in Lean 4 against Mathlib: no commutativity used, the empty overlaps' equations derived, the cyclic hypothesis doing no work below the reading of the Čech system as a flow.

**The Ring and the Chord** (order 25, v0.1). Both consequences fail off the cycle. The coefficient ring matters (Peres–Mermin and GHZ obstructed over ℤ, invisible over ℚ — parity is 2-torsion). On no-signalling models the invariant is nearly blind; on signalling ones it sees most; on a K₄ holarchy with tolerance gates it sees everything. "A poor test of physics and a good test of gates." Compute over ℤ.

**Coutility** (order 18, v0.2). Open games supply the composition operation *Gluing* declined to. Gate on coutility and every constituent gate is inherited (a recommendation, not a result). Compositionality of an equilibrium predicate does not imply compositionality of an admissibility predicate (Prop 4.1). Names the "three relocations" pattern.

### 2.5 The papers — Sybil, richness, closure

**The Multiplicity Freedom** (order 12, v0.5). Sybil cost is arithmetic, not capability, so it is the one provable fragment. Cap theorem N ≤ ⌊C/Γ⌋; conjunction amplifies the floor by 1 + (K−1)ι; only convexity above the gate punishes splitting. Conditions C1 (participation) and C2 (tolerance below spectral gap) required on H⁰. The cap "is not a cap on a modular substrate." Every quantitative claim is a function of ι, still unformalised.

**Requisite Richness** (order 14, v0.2). Ashby supplies a floor on ρ set by the adversary's strategy variety. With *Sign and Work*'s ceiling, ρ is squeezed from both sides by the same adversary and the feasible interval may be empty. The commensurability of the unit is "load-bearing" and unresolved.

**A Consistent Fiction** (order 17, v0.2). Maturana–Varela applied operationally. A coherence mechanism is operationally closed; its fiction space has dimension d (the stalk dimension), measured; closing it costs d scalars of external contact per component, which the anchors by rule do not supply. "The program has chosen closure, knowingly, and has never priced the choice." Usage does not buy contact either: satisfaction is maximised at exactly zero contact. The inward axis is the only available form of structural coupling, pending a construction.

**The Census of Errors** (order 32, v0.1, drafted 3 October 2026, recovered 6 November). Sleeping Beauty's camps disagree about the census, not the coin, and a mechanism that pays agreement is a thirder. Theorem 1: any global section is a Nash equilibrium of every coherence-only mechanism and pays more than honesty. A witness, for payment, is an independence class of errors; the cluster-leave-out rule pays each class once, so a copy and its original share one payment. Concealment pays only while tasks per epoch are below the square of copies per item. A consistent lie prevails nowhere to 45% of identities and everywhere at 50%: past half, the two worlds are formally interchangeable and only the price of identity remains. *The Multiplicity Freedom* proves the cap; this builds the payment rule beneath it. Its simulator is not in the repository and the paper says so.

### 2.6 The papers — durability and the null specimen

**Borrowed Again** (order 19, v0.1). Corrects Volume IV's empirical claim: NIST's primary standard (ML-DSA) is module-lattice *structure*; the migration moves between structures, not to bulk. The price of derivation is measurable in bytes (2–10×). A signature's trace gap is ≈ 1 for "the holder assented" and ≈ 0 for anything broader; "the gap between those two numbers is where mechanisms leak."

**The Barrowverse** (order 28, v0.1). Adopts the Bitcoin-favourable position at full scope and points it at a marketplace of meme artefacts. Market readings are signs (τ ≤ 0, sometimes negative). Depth under the proof-of-work mound grades at τ = d/κ, the corpus's first reading above one — and it is a free ride: socially the gap is one, the inscriber's is one over its share of the bill. Four recorded breaks. Opens a build-or-Volume-VI fork.

**The Involuntary Ledger** (order 30, v0.1, written 10 August 2026, admitted 7 November). Measure the civilisation as if it could not be asked. Energy is the one outward magnitude whose certifiable shadow — dissipation — is the substrate; four residue channels (thermal, chemical, nuclear, structural), the atmosphere as a double-entry ledger nobody built; the planetary aggregate is relocation-proof and the denominator of a capacity currency measurable without an oracle, while attribution below it is not; the logarithmic rung forgives a ten-percent error to four parts in a thousand. Reads its conjunction as multiplicative, which the exclusion line later denied; not revised. *Kar-Coin* v0.4 records an earlier payment on the same debt, and the paper's "first payment" is struck.

**What the Burn Buys** (order 31, v0.1, written 10 August 2026, admitted 7 November). Bitcoin's heat is the certificate, not the tax; the burn pays for four jobs at once — randomness, ordering, Sybil pricing, unforgeability — and the four anchors re-let them severally, so irreducible heat collapses to a Sybil floor times identity churn plus one wound clock. Landauer prices erasure, not computation, so proof of work certifies cost and only contingently heat; the dissipation floor is respecified as a cost floor. A menu of honest minables with law-pinned trace gaps; a reactor-flux mint worked as the example, compressed-oracle and not oracle-free. Not revised against the exclusion line.

### 2.7 The two papers of 6 November 2026

**Key and Census** (order 33, v0.1). Every Goodhart-asymptotic sequence terminates in a preimage check; every Sybil-asymptotic sequence terminates in a signature; the two limits meet at an integer, the Key. The corpus had the reduction as far as the anchor (*One's Own Anchor*'s theorem, *Independent and Expensive*'s "K anchors wearing readings"); this takes the anchors to their own limit, where each is a preimage or a key. The derivations of value are severed in the limit; the presheaf of derivations has no global section; H¹ ≠ 0, exhibited and not computed. Its "Census" is the cocycle written out, the word borrowed from *The Census of Errors*' count of witnesses for a count of reasons. Cites *The Multiplicity Freedom* for the cap and *The Census of Errors* for the cliff, and owns the reduction of the primitive to a key.

**The Extent of Value** (order 34, v0.1). Takes [v] ∈ H¹ as the definition of value and follows it: a difference never a level (numéraire as local trivialisation, cocycle condition as no-arbitrage); monodromy around the key as a puncture, the loop run around the four substrates whose nerve is a cycle; requires two valuers; has a canonical harmonic representative once overlaps are weighted — Proof by Resonance on the nerve of the papers, relocating the choice onto the weights as Conjecture R predicts; has finite dimension b₁, equal to one for the four substrates, which is Volume V's deflation as a number, and which stabilises at the good cover, a testable prediction about the Census; vanishes for the monologue and the dispersal, so the cover is the lever for value as for security.

Both were written before the corpus above was read in full; §6 records what they had to correct, and the corrections are made in the landed versions.

## 3. What stands

Results the corpus currently treats as established, each with the paper that owns it.

1. The operator is well-defined and recurs at every layer it has been applied to, including cryptographic hardness (*Borrowed Hardness*, qualified by *Borrowed Again*).
2. The thermodynamics of vectorised money run and hold under test (*Admitted or Refused*).
3. Richness is a fractional spectral dimension, d_s ≈ 1.61, continuously tunable with cross-link density (*Admitted or Refused*; *Gluing the Gates* Obs. 5.1).
4. The Sybil cap theorem and its amplification formula (*The Multiplicity Freedom*), subject to C1, C2, and a formalised ι.
5. The trace gap τ is well-defined, calibrated on proof of work at 1.054, and bounded above by 1 − b₁/\|E\| under output anchors (*Sign and Work*).
6. The exclusion principle and the budget law: no two projections of one pool are both independent and sound; the only escape is a second pool, and the second pool was built and matched the arithmetic to the decimal (*Independent and Expensive*; *The Second Pool*).
7. Every declared-frame reading evidences nothing — a theorem, not a measurement (*One's Own Anchor* Thm 3.4).
8. One reading survives: fitted restriction maps gated on residuals, under commit-before-compare, priced on the coalition's boundary (*One's Own Anchor*; *A World with Memory*).
9. Composition failure is contextuality; the Čech obstruction computes on gate scenarios without an affine hypothesis; it is a detector, with a measured miss rate, and ring-dependent off the cycle (*No Global Section*; *The Obstruction, Computed*; *The Ring and the Chord*).
10. The cover is the lever: redrawing comparisons restores a Sybil floor of half of honest at the cost of richness (*Every Basis at Once*).
11. The pool is conditional entropy; ι and τ have information-theoretic forms meeting all four constraints (*A World with Memory*) — the candidate for P6, not its discharge.
12. A reading above one exists, and it is depth under a mound of work, and it is a free ride on the subsidy (*The Barrowverse*).
13. A payment rule under which copies earn one share, Sybil-proof in the limit of observation, with an impossibility at half the identities (*The Census of Errors*) — measured on an instrument that is not in the repository, so standing as reported and not as reproduced.

## 4. What has been deflated

Claims that were made in the corpus and have since been refuted, struck, or reduced, with the instrument that did it.

- Coherence as a security substrate. The sheaf carries no forgery resistance; the gap is bought by anchors (*Gauge-Fixing* §5.1, *Sign and Work* §5.3, *PoC* v0.5).
- Proof by Resonance as an independent second projection. Not independent in the static case; free to forge in the temporal case; rewards stasis at 5× (*Combination Proofs* v0.4, *PoC* v0.5).
- The multiplication claim as a theorem. Still Prop 3.1, informal, with the leading-order clause open.
- The gap hierarchy. A degeneracy artefact (*Admitted or Refused*).
- Four substrates. One substrate, residue, in four costumes (*What Cannot Be Helped*).
- Vanishing H¹ as a certificate. A detector; silence is not evidence of absence (*No Global Section*).
- The Sybil cap on a modular substrate. Not a cap; the boundary halves per level and the adversary picks the level (*One's Own Anchor*, *The Budget Across Levels*).
- Post-quantum migration as a move to derived hardness. A move between structures (*Borrowed Again*).
- Usage as structural coupling. Refuted by measurement; closure is rewarded (*A Consistent Fiction* §6.1).
- Volume I's claim that negentropy escapes the first two derivations. Unsupported (*The Barrowverse*).
- Contextuality as a resource. Closed: commitment dominates (*Every Basis at Once*).

## 5. The through-lines

Five sentences the plan now states as the program's findings, in the order they were earned.

- **Formalisms relocate difficulties; they do not dissolve them.** Named Conjecture R. Eleven confirmations at the last count, nothing scheduled to refute it.
- **The substrate does the work the mechanism was credited with.** A theorem for declared-frame readings.
- **The cover is the lever.** Who compares with whom is the only design variable that moved a security number.
- **What survives is information, not work.** The surviving reading measures innovation given the record.
- **The boundary is the whole of the price.** And the adversary chooses the level, so the boundary can be made small.

Two more, from the two papers of 6 November, offered for the list and not yet earned by measurement:

- **The anchors are keys.** Every anchor, taken to its limit, is a preimage check or a signature; "K anchors wearing readings" is K integers wearing readings.
- **Value is the obstruction.** What remains when the keys are removed is the class in H¹ of the derivations, and it has dimension one — which is the deflation of Volume V stated cohomologically.

## 8. The island

The paragraph the corpus currently reduces to. It is the thing a reader should be able to carry away, and it is the thing the next paper has to be written against.

One rule, applied thirty-four times. Four substrates named; one found. Every security property the mechanism claimed has been traced by measurement to its anchors, and the anchors, in the limit, are integers: a preimage or a signature, possessed or not. Copies are paid as one witness, and at half the identities the copies are the world. One reading survives the tracing, and it measures contact with a world rather than work, on a boundary the adversary can shrink to nothing by choosing a level, restored only by redrawing the cover at the cost of the richness the cover was supposed to carry. The derivations of value in the anthology are each locally valid and none is the restriction of a global one; the obstruction to their gluing is a class of dimension one; the corpus names that class value and has nothing else to point to under the word. Composition failure is contextuality and the invariant that detects it is a detector and not a certificate. Formalisms relocate difficulties with precision and have not dissolved one. Nothing is built. The security proof recedes. The program continues, because the obstruction is the content and the cover is still being written.
