## Abstract

*Kar-Coin* v0.3 left the capacity substrate with a named debt: to show how much of a civilization's outward magnitude can be made to *follow* from the artifacts it must leave behind, so that the coin reads the flow through its residue rather than through its report. This paper makes the first payment, for the first and most legible coordinate — energy capture — and its instrument is an inversion. Kardashev's scale was an astronomer's yardstick, designed in 1964 for civilizations that could not be interrogated; the waste-heat searches that followed it are an existing methodology of oracle-free civilizational energy metrology, and the oracle problem entered the program's sketch only when the measurement moved indoors, to meters and inspectors and forms. The governing principle is therefore: **measure the civilization as if it could not be asked.** The theory that results rests on the two laws. Conservation guarantees that no generated joule fails to arrive somewhere; dissipation guarantees that every used joule terminates as heat. Energy is thereby the unique outward magnitude for which the negentropy volume's obstruction inverts into an asset — the shadow a proof can catch (dissipation, which is self-verifying) *is* the substrate being measured (throughput), up to storage. Four residue channels follow: **thermal** (all use ends as heat; complete by the second law, sound by the first; trace gap τ = 1 exactly, since faking a joule of heat costs a joule), **chemical** (combustion writes the atmosphere in double entry — the carbon column rises and the oxygen column falls in fuel-specific stoichiometric ratios, with radiocarbon depletion as the provenance mark of fossil origin), **nuclear** (fission emits an antineutrino flux proportional to power that no shielding stops and no known process counterfeits), and **structural** (capture infrastructure is the inward-axis artifact *Kar-Coin* already admitted — persistent, orbitally visible, re-measurable by anyone). Their conjunction is a Combination Proof of order four with cross-channel stoichiometric checksums, publicity-positive in the framework's sense. The central architectural result is a split: at the **planetary aggregate** the relocation attack dissolves, because the planet is the one control volume no adversary can redraw — only radiation crosses it, and radiation is the thermal channel read from outside — so the *denominator* of a capacity currency is measurable now, without an oracle, from residues alone; while **attribution** below the aggregate reintroduces boundaries and remains partially oracle-bound. The logarithmic rung then forgives the linear error: σ_K ≈ 0.043 · (σ_P/P), so a ten-percent measurement of planetary power reads the Kardashev coordinate to four parts in a thousand. A companion working document (`_plan/planetary-meter.md`) carries the feasibility audit, instrument by instrument.

## 1. The Debt, and an Inversion

The capacity volume arrived at its oracle honestly. A megawatt-hour is a flow; a flow is gone the moment it has passed; what remains is the record, and a record is a report, and a report needs a reporter — the meter, the inspector, the certificate, the form. Every one of these is a point where the mechanism stops measuring and starts trusting, and *Kar-Coin* concluded that the outward ladder inherits the oracle by the direction it points. The volume then relocated the debt rather than discharging it: the design problem is to make the outward magnitude *follow* from what the civilization cannot help leaving behind.

The relocation contains its own solution, and the solution was present at the substrate's birth. Kardashev proposed the scale in a paper about detecting transmissions from civilizations across interstellar distances — civilizations that could not be asked, audited, or subpoenaed, whose meters no one would ever read and whose institutions no one would ever trust, because no channel to them existed except the physics they could not help emitting. The scale was *born* oracle-free. The waste-heat searches that grew from it — Dyson's infrared excess, the surveys that scan galaxies for the thermal signature of use — are a working methodology for measuring a civilization's energy budget under the assumption that testimony is unavailable in principle.

The oracle problem entered only when the measurement moved indoors. Asked to measure *ourselves*, the sketch reached by habit for the instruments a civilization uses on its members — meters, filings, attestations — and inherited their trust structure. The astronomer's posture was available the whole time. A civilization is a physical process; a physical process cannot keep the books it would need to keep to lie to a bolometer; and the honest meter for a civilization is the one that would be pointed at it by an observer it cannot answer.

The principle this paper works under is therefore stated once and governs everything after it: **measure the civilization as if it could not be asked.**

## 2. Conservation Is the Auditor

The program has been here before, from the other side, and the reversal is the theoretical heart of the paper.

*Gauge-Fixing the Section Space* v0.2 §1 fixed the direction of the arrow: one can prove that free energy was *spent* — dissipation is self-verifying, its physical correlate following from thermodynamics without a witness — but one cannot prove that order was *made*. That was the obstruction, because the negentropy substrate wanted to certify the making. The capacity substrate wants to certify the spending. The arrow has not turned; the mechanism has walked around to its point. For energy throughput, the shadow a proof can catch and the substrate to be measured are the same quantity: what a civilization generates, it uses; what it uses, the second law terminates as heat; and heat is the one physical claim the canon of proof was already able to certify. Among the outward magnitudes — energy, computation, coordination, longevity — energy is the *unique* one for which unwitnessability fails to bite, because it is the one whose residue is guaranteed by law rather than by design.

Two laws divide the labour. The first law is the completeness half of Volume V's biconditional: no joule can be generated without arriving somewhere — as work, as storage, or as heat — so the residue record has no gaps for a generator to hide in. The second law is the terminal clause: every use, however indirect, degrades to dissipation, so the record's final column is written in a single currency. Between them they close the audit the way no institution can: not because anyone checks, but because there is nothing else the energy could have done.

There is one term the identity carries and honesty must carry with it: storage. Energy generated and banked — in reservoirs, in fuels synthesized and held, in the lattice of built things — has not yet dissipated, and a throughput meter reads it late. At civilizational scale the storage term is small against the flow and bounded by the visible stock of stores, but it is a term, and the theory owes it a line in every budget the companion document draws.

## 3. The Four Channels

A civilization's energy throughput casts residues in four channels, and they are unlike enough to be worth naming separately, because their independence is what §6 gates on.

**Thermal.** All use terminates as heat. The channel is *complete* — no use without dissipation, by the second law — and *sound* — no dissipation without expenditure, by the first — and it is the only channel with both properties unconditionally. Its trace gap is exactly one: to counterfeit a joule of waste heat, the forger must dissipate a joule, and dissipating a joule requires having a joule, which is the claim. The residue is the expenditure; the biconditional is an identity. Read at the point of use, the channel sees everything, whatever the source; read as net planetary flux, it sees only *added* energy — fossil and fission — since captured sunlight is diversion of a flux already arriving. The channel's weakness is not theoretical but instrumental, and §7 states it without softening.

**Chemical.** Combustion writes the atmosphere, and it writes in double entry. Burning carbon raises the carbon dioxide column and draws down the oxygen column, in stoichiometric ratios fixed by the fuel — roughly 1.2 moles of oxygen per mole of carbon dioxide for coal, near 2 for methane — and the ratios are a checksum no bookkeeper maintains. The provenance mark comes free: fossil carbon has been dead for geological time, its radiocarbon long decayed, so the combustion of the seam dilutes the atmosphere's carbon-14 in a way the burning of last year's forest does not. The channel certifies *fossil* throughput specifically — which is, at this writing, the large majority of the quantity — and its trace gap is high: writing both books in the right ratio with the right isotopic signature, without combustion, means performing something indistinguishable from combustion at comparable cost. The known partial forgeries fail single books and are caught by the other: calcining limestone emits radiocarbon-dead carbon dioxide while drawing no oxygen; sequestration hides the carbon entry while the oxygen drawdown stands recorded, since capture takes the exhaust and not the intake.

**Nuclear.** A fission reactor emits antineutrinos in proportion to its thermal power — of order 10²⁰ per second per gigawatt — and the flux serves as a power meter with two properties no other channel has. It cannot be shielded, because the interaction length of an antineutrino is measured in light-years of lead; and it cannot be counterfeited, because no known process produces the reactor spectrum except fission itself. The trace gap is one by physics: to fake the flux is to run the reactor. The channel's limit is reach — present detectors read single reactors at kilometres and national fleets at hundreds of kilometres, and the flux falls as the square of the distance — and coverage, since fission is a modest fraction of the total. It is the sharpest per-joule instrument in the set and the narrowest.

**Structural.** Capture infrastructure persists. Dams, turbine fields, panel arrays, cooling towers are artifacts in exactly the sense *Kar-Coin*'s inward axis demanded: they remain after the flow has passed, they are visible from orbit to any party with optics, and they return the same answer to every observer without once being trusted. The channel is the coverage complement of the chemical one — renewable capture burns nothing and adds no net heat, so its residue is the *equipment* — and it is the weak channel alone: infrastructure certifies capacity, not throughput, and a built array generating nothing is a residue without a flow. Alone, its trace gap is the cost of construction against the value of the claim, which no law fixes at one. In conjunction it is disciplined by the others: claimed generation must reappear downstream as somebody's dissipation.

## 4. The Ledger Nobody Built

The chemical channel deserves its own section, because it is not merely a residue but a *ledger*, and the program should recognize the species.

The atmosphere is globally mixed on a timescale of about a year — weeks to months within a hemisphere, a year across the equator. Every combustion event on the planet posts to it; the posting cannot be prevented, addressed selectively, or recalled; and after one mixing time every sampling station on Earth holds the same integrated record, not because the stations trust one another but because they draw from the same well-stirred reservoir. Read against the program's vocabulary the structure is exact. The ledger is *append-only*: entries are molecules, and un-emitting them is precisely the erasure the second law prices. It is *un-forkable*: there is one atmosphere, and a party wishing to present a divergent history must present divergent air. Its *consensus mechanism* is turbulent mixing — agreement enforced by advection rather than by protocol, at no one's expense. Its *block time* is the interhemispheric mixing time. And its read access is universal in the strongest sense the anthology has encountered: the instrument of audit is a flask and a spectrometer, and the record answers any party, at any latitude, without permission.

It keeps double books — the carbon column and the oxygen column, reconciled per fuel by stoichiometry, with radiocarbon as the provenance mark — and this is what elevates it from residue to ledger: single-entry forgeries fail reconciliation, as §3 catalogued.

Two qualifications keep the section honest. The ledger has a *slow second copy*: the ocean takes up a large fraction of what the air receives — the airborne fraction of emitted carbon is roughly two-fifths — so the atmospheric stock understates the cumulative flow, and the marine ledger that holds the remainder is real but slower and costlier to read. And the ledger is read *through a model*: inferring the flux from the stock's growth requires the sink partition, and correcting the oxygen book requires the ocean's own seasonal breathing. The residue is model-free; the reading of it is not, and a model maintained by a scientific community is an institution of a softer kind — softer, because its inputs are public and its disagreements are published and any party may re-derive it, but an institution nonetheless. The theory records the dependence rather than waving at it: the chemical channel is oracle-free in its *record* and community-refereed in its *interpretation*, and the distance between those two is where its residual trust lives.

## 5. The Boundary Nobody Draws

The relocation attack has shadowed the program since the preservation volume: entropy accounting requires a boundary, the boundary is the adversary's free parameter, and for any control volume flattering the claim there is a redrawing that deflates it. *Kar-Coin* watched the attack return wearing a solar panel — energy captured *here*, or bought, relabelled, and double-counted across a border drawn for the purpose.

At the planetary aggregate the attack dissolves, and the dissolution is structural rather than fortunate. The planet is a control volume nobody drew and nobody can redraw: its boundary is crossed by radiation and essentially nothing else, every terrestrial transfer — every export, purchase, relabelling, every boundary game an accountant can play — nets to zero inside it, and the one flux that does cross is the thermal channel read from outside, which is where this paper began. The adversary's free parameter was the boundary; the planet is the boundary that is nobody's parameter. Aggregate throughput is therefore relocation-proof by construction, and the oracle problem for civilizational energy was never a *measurement* problem. It was an *attribution* problem wearing one.

The split does real architectural work, because the two halves are needed by different parts of a capacity currency. The **denominator** — the rung, the unit of account, the civilization's position on the ladder — is a property of the aggregate, and the aggregate is what the residue channels measure without an oracle. The **minting** — which agent contributed what, and is owed what — requires boundaries inside the civilization, and inside the civilization the relocation attack lives on, tamed only where generation is a point source resolvable by an instrument (a plant's thermal plume, a fleet's antineutrino flux, a flare's radiance) and untamed where it is distributed. The theory's claim is calibrated accordingly: the denominator is payable now; the minting remains partially oracle-bound, and the companion document maps the attribution ladder rung by rung.

One property of the denominator deserves promotion from arithmetic to architecture. The Kardashev coordinate is logarithmic — K = (log₁₀ P − 6)/10, on Sagan's interpolation — so its error responds to the *relative* error of the power measurement, damped by the decade: σ_K ≈ σ_P/P · 1/(10 ln 10) ≈ 0.043 · (σ_P/P). A ten-percent measurement of planetary power — crude by any metrological standard — fixes K to about ±0.004; even a factor-of-two error moves the coordinate by only ±0.03. The grand scale is also the forgiving one: log denomination is precisely the encoding under which planetary metrology as it exists today already suffices for a stable unit of account. The rung does not ask the meter to be sharp. It asks it to be honest about which decade the civilization stands in, and decades are hard to counterfeit.

## 6. The Conjunction

Assembled, the four channels are a Combination Proof in the framework's exact sense, and the reading discharges its conditions one by one. The projections are *verifiably independent*: thermal, chemical, nuclear, and structural residues are carried by different physics — radiation, stoichiometry, weak interaction, persistence of built matter — and an adversary's apparatus for forging one contributes nothing toward forging another. The reward gates on the *conjunction*: a fossil claim must appear in the heat and in both atmospheric books at the right stoichiometry with the right isotopic mark; a fission claim in the heat and the flux; a solar claim in the structure and in the *absence* of the chemical entry, with its consumption reappearing as somebody's dissipation. The fake-cost is multiplicative across channels whose individual gaps are already, for three of the four, pinned at or near one by physical law. And the arrangement is *publicity-positive*: publishing the channel list, the stoichiometric checksums, and the station network raises the forger's burden — every disclosed cross-check is one more book to cook in concert — while the honest generator's cost does not move, since the honest generator's residues are being emitted anyway, involuntarily, which is the entire content of Volume V's word.

The discipline of the anchor paper carries over unamended: no channel is asked to certify past its competence. Thermal certifies expenditure, not source; chemical certifies fossil combustion, not usefulness; the flux certifies fission power, not its distribution; structure certifies capacity, never throughput. Each channel stretched past its clause becomes a proxy, and the program has said what happens to proxies.

## 7. What Is Declined

Five deflations, each load-bearing.

**Usefulness is not measured, and the omission is the design.** The channels read throughput — joules through the civilizational boundary — and a civilization burning its forests for nothing reads as throughput, because it is. The temptation to weight the measurement by worth is the failed useful-work derivation returning at planetary scale, and it is declined for the reason the first volume gave: the verifier of usefulness is an oracle, and the honest meter reads magnitude and leaves worth to the conjunction with the other capacity coordinates, where *Kar-Coin* put it.

**The best channel is temporary.** The chemical ledger is, at this writing, the sharpest instrument in the set — and it covers the fossil fraction, which decarbonization exists to shrink. The residue-richest energy regime is the one being exited: as capture migrates from the seam to the sun, the involuntary ledger's coverage decays toward the structural channel, which is the weakest alone, and the thermal channel, which is the strongest in principle and the least sensitive in practice. The meter's composition must migrate with the civilization it measures, and a mechanism that froze today's channel weights would be measuring the century it was born in.

**The permanent channel cannot yet see the aggregate.** Anthropogenic dissipation is of order twenty terawatts — about four hundredths of a watt per square metre of Earth — while the planet's radiative books are measured to absolute accuracies coarser than that by more than an order of magnitude, and the net imbalance those books do resolve is dominated by the greenhouse term, thirty times larger. The thermal channel today is attribution-grade at point sources and blind at the aggregate; its global reading accumulates only slowly, in the ocean's heat content. The channel on which the theory's completeness rests is the one whose instrument is not yet built, and the companion document prices the gap rather than assuming it closed.

**The reading of the ledger is community-refereed.** §4 declared the model dependence and it is repeated here as a limit: the record is oracle-free, the flux inference is not, and the residual trust in the chemical channel is the sink model — public, replicable, contested in the open, and still a thing maintained by people. The honest statement is that the channel replaces *asked institutions* with *checkable ones*, which is a real reduction and not an elimination.

**Residue answers counterfeit, not concealment.** The theory is built against overstatement — inflating the denominator, minting against joules never generated — and there the biconditionals bind. Understatement is the other direction: a party generating in secret and hiding the residues. Heat can be delayed into storage but storage grows; combustion can be moved but not unwritten; and yet the theory claims no completeness against a determined concealer, and Volume V's warning stands — residue says nothing about denial. For a *denominator* the concealment attack deflates rather than inflates, which is the safer failure for a currency; the paper records the asymmetry without pretending it is a proof.

## 8. Coda

The program's founding operator, applied to metrology, reads: stop asking the meter and stake what the meter's reading is derived from. A report of a megawatt-hour derives from combustion that drew the sky's oxygen, from fission that lit the ground with antineutrinos, from heat that is still leaving, from equipment that is still standing. The report can be forged and the derivation cannot, and the difference between them is four channels wide and one planet deep.

What this paper adds to the capacity volume is the discovery that its hardest coordinate was its most measurable — that the oracle stood not between the mechanism and the joules but between the mechanism and the *names on* the joules, and that a unit of account needs no names. A civilization cannot keep its throughput secret from its own sky, any more than the walker of Volume V could cross the grass and leave it standing. The flame confesses; the confession is stoichiometric; the transcript is the air.

The universe spends itself, and a civilization is the local, magnificent refusal to be spent — but the refusal is financed, every joule of it, and the financing throws shadows no borrower can suppress. Value denominated against the rung was the grandest claim in the program and the least verifiable as sketched. Read through the involuntary ledger, it is still the grandest, and it is the one whose meter was running before the program began, before any mechanism existed to read it — the one meter, in the whole anthology, that no one will ever have to be asked to trust, because no one was ever asked to build it.
